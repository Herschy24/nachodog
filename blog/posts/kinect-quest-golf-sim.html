A golf simulator from a Kinect and a Quest

An old Xbox Kinect tracks my body and swing, and a Meta Quest puts me on the course.

Proper golf simulators cost thousands. I had an old Xbox Kinect gathering dust and a Meta Quest, and wanted to see how close I could get to a home simulator by combining them.

Why combine the two?

The Quest is great at putting you somewhere else and tracking your hands, but a golf swing is a whole-body motion. The Kinect was built for exactly that: full-body skeleton tracking from across the room. The idea was to let each device do what it's good at.
