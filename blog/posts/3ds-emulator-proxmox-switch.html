Playing 3DS games on a Switch, via Proxmox

A 3DS emulator running in my homelab, streamed into the Nintendo Switch's web browser.

The Switch has no 3DS backwards compatibility, but it does have a (hidden) web browser. My homelab runs Proxmox. Put those together and you get 3DS games running on a server in the cupboard and played from the couch on a Switch.

Note: only play games you own, dumped from your own cartridges. This post covers the setup, not where to get games.
